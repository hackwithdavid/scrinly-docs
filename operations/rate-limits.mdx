---
title: "Host rate limits"
description: "Understand host-aware concurrency and safe retry behavior."
---

Browser work is limited by normalized target host so one origin cannot consume all browser capacity. The limiter applies to synchronous requests, queue consumers, batches, and monitor captures.

A saturated host returns `429` with `Retry-After`. Successful requests that waited may include `X-Scrinly-RateLimit-Bucket` and `X-Scrinly-RateLimit-Waited`; the bucket is a safe normalized identifier, not a copy of the submitted URL.

Spread unrelated work across the queue and use exponential backoff with jitter. Retrying immediately at high concurrency increases contention and can extend the delay. Status polling is read-only and does not consume a browser slot.

Provider rate limits for Visual Style Guide generation are normalized into typed `style_guide_*` errors. Scrinly does not silently fall back to another provider or model.
