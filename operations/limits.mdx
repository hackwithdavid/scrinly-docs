Limits are validation ceilings, not performance targets. Full-page screenshots, script-heavy sites, large DOMs, and stylesheet-heavy design extraction can take longer than simple snapshots.

## Pagination and truncation

Crawl pages use a cursor. Send the returned cursor unchanged on the next request rather than constructing offsets.

Async job results must fit in D1. When a result exceeds about 1.5 MB, Scrinly removes large optional fields in a deterministic order and records their names in `result.truncated`. Prefer object storage when you need complete large artifacts.
