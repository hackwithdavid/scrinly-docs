## Public API surface

The public API is intentionally focused: screenshots, screenshot batches, visual diffs, monitors, asynchronous status, and usage. Structured page extraction, site-wide traversal, generic scheduling, and recovery tooling are internal capabilities and are not customer APIs.

Every customer route uses a Scrinly bearer key. Images stored by Scrinly or in your own provider are returned through direct provider URLs; Scrinly does not proxy those assets.

<Note>
  Capture only publicly accessible pages or pages you are authorized to access. Headers, cookies, interactions, and customer-storage credentials belong in HTTPS headers or JSON bodies, never in URLs or client-side code.
</Note>
